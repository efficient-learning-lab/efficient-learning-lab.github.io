Getting Involved

To apply for the graduate or internship program at our lab, please send an email to Hankook with your transcript and CV.

Lab Culture

We are committed to building our culture based on the following principles:
  • Mutual respect
  • A research-focused environment
  • Weekly individual meetings

Internship Program

We highly encourage undergraduate students to experience a lab internship before applying for the graduate program. During the internship, you will not only study deep learning, but also learn how to do coding and how to collaborate effectively.
